Getting started

Three steps, and the first one takes two minutes.

Send a request

Fill in the short form with your name, contact details, and what you’re looking for help with. Fill out our short contact form with your name, contact details, and a few sentences about what you are looking for.

Free consultation call

We’ll reply to arrange a free 15-minute call to hear what’s going on, answer your questions, and match you with the right therapist.

Begin sessions

If it’s a good fit, we schedule your first full session by secure video, at a time that fits around your work and family.
